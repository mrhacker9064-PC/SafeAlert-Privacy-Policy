# Privacy Policy for SafeAlert Pro **Last Updated:** October 17, 2026 SafeAlert Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to protect your data when you use the SafeAlert Pro mobile application. --- ### 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It #### a. Location Information (Approximate and Precise) - **Data Collected:** Real-time GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude). - **Purpose:** Location data is collected to display your position on the map and to share your live coordinates with designated emergency contacts via SMS or messaging apps during an SOS trigger. - **Background Location:** If enabled, location data may be accessed in the background strictly to facilitate live location tracking and safety verification when the SOS feature or sensor triggers are activated. #### b. Emergency Contacts and Phone Data - **Data Collected:** Phone numbers and contact names added to the Emergency Contacts directory. - **Purpose:** Used strictly to dispatch emergency SOS messages, initiate direct phone calls, or dial emergency helplines (Police, Ambulance, Women Safety Helpline). We do not upload your full address book to any external servers. #### c. Camera and Video Recording - **Data Collected:** Video captures recorded through the device camera. - **Purpose:** The app accesses the camera solely upon user initiation to record emergency evidence via the Emergency Toolkit. Recorded videos remain stored on your local device storage unless explicitly shared by you. #### d. Microphone and Audio Data - **Data Collected:** Audio recordings and voice keyword audio streams. - **Purpose:** 1. Manual audio recording within the Emergency Toolkit to record emergency evidence. 2. Voice SOS feature to recognize pre-configured trigger phrases (e.g., "help", "save me", "danger", "bachao"). Voice recognition processing is performed locally to detect safety trigger phrases. #### e. Sensor Data (Accelerometer / Motion) - **Data Collected:** Device motion and shake sensor readings. - **Purpose:** To detect rapid movement or shaking when "Shake Phone SOS" is enabled, triggering the emergency alert protocol. #### f. Account and Authentication Information - **Data Collected:** Email address, user account identifiers, and medical emergency information (such as Blood Group). - **Purpose:** Used for user account creation, secure login, password reset requests, session management, and displaying user medical details to first responders in an emergency. --- ### 2. Device Permissions Required To deliver core emergency features, SafeAlert Pro requests the following sensitive Android permissions: - `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` & `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`: To fetch precise coordinates for emergency alerts. - `CAMERA`: To capture emergency video evidence. - `RECORD_AUDIO`: To capture audio recordings and enable voice emergency detection. - `CALL_PHONE` / `DIAL`: To initiate immediate calls to emergency helplines or saved contacts. - `SEND_SMS`: To transmit emergency alert texts with your location link. All permissions require explicit user consent and can be revoked at any time through the device system settings, though core safety features will be disabled. --- ### 3. Data Storage, Retention, and Security - **Local Storage:** Audio recordings, video recordings, and emergency contact lists are stored locally on your device sandbox. - **Security Protocols:** All authentication operations (login and password management) and data in transit use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS). - **No Data Sale:** We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information, emergency contacts, or location history to any third-party data brokers or advertisers. --- ### 4. Third-Party Services SafeAlert Pro may integrate trusted standard third-party services: - **Google Play Services / Google Maps API:** To render maps and fetch accurate geolocation coordinates. - **Authentication Providers (e.g., Firebase Auth):** To authenticate user accounts securely. These third-party providers process data in compliance with their respective privacy policies. --- ### 5. Account Deletion and Data Management You have full control over your personal data: - You can edit or delete emergency contacts, update your medical profile, or modify voice keywords directly inside the app settings. - If you wish to delete your account and associated registration data permanently, you may request deletion by contacting us at our support email. --- ### 6. Children's Privacy SafeAlert Pro is not designed for or targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. --- ### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published here with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to review this page periodically. --- ### 8.
